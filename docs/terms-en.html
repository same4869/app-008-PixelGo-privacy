1. Service Description

PixelGo is a location-based exploration game. It uses GPS to transform your walking routes into pixel tiles on a map, offering gamified experiences including territory conquest, landmark discovery, and leaderboard competition.

2. Accounts & Data

  • PixelGo does not require account registration. Apple ID / Game Center serves as your identity.
  • Your exploration data is stored locally on your device by default. You have full control over your data.
  • You can reset or delete your exploration data at any time in Settings.

3. Paid Services

  • Pro is a one-time non-consumable in-app purchase processed through the Apple App Store.
